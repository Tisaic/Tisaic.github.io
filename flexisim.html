One machine, one function block. A two-link arm with flexible links and geared joints, under its conventional joint servo. FB_AutoFF is called once per scan: press Commission and it measures the machine, fits its own feedforward, keeps what helps and refuses what does not — then trims the joint setpoints for ever after. The ghost underneath is the same machine without the block.
